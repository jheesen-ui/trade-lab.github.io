# Privacy Policy — AI T-Lab Backup

**Last updated:** October 26, 2026

AI Trading Lab Backup is a personal backup application operated for private use.

**Google Drive access:** The application uses the Google Drive API with the `drive.file` permission to create, access and maintain files it is authorized to manage.

**Data storage:** Research backup data is encrypted locally using Restic before being transferred to Google Drive. The Google OAuth configuration is stored locally on the operator's device.

**Data sharing:** The application is not intended to sell user data or share it with advertisers.

**Retention and deletion:** Encrypted backup files are retained and deleted according to backup retention settings established by the operator.

**Contact:** The operator may be contacted through the support email shown on the application's Google OAuth consent screen.
